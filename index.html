About us

A small group with a big passion.

Northbound TCG is a private, independent playgroup. We are not a registered association and do not pursue any commercial interests.

We play Riftbound for fun, share ideas about decks, test new strategies, and occasionally attend local events together.

What matters most to us is fairness, mutual respect, and enjoying our shared hobby together.
